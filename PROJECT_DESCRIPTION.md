# Project Description

A web application for booking dental appointments. Dental clinics publish their availability, and patients request appointments through a public link, with no account needed.

## Users

- **Clinics** register and manage their own account. Many clinics can use the system, each with its own data.
- **Patients** book through a clinic's link. They do not register or sign in.

## Clinic features

- Register and sign in to a clinic account.
- Add the dentists who work at the clinic. Each dentist has their own calendar.
- Define services, each with its own duration (for example a check-up or a filling).
- Set working hours and breaks. The system offers free slots based on them and on the duration of the chosen service.
- Get a unique public booking link to share on Instagram or the clinic's website.
- See who requested which slot and when.
- Approve or decline each request, and assign a dentist on approval.
- Reschedule or cancel existing appointments.

## Patient booking flow

1. Open the clinic's booking link.
2. Choose a service and a free slot.
3. Enter email, name and phone number.
4. Submit the request. It stays pending until the clinic approves or declines it.

Patients can cancel or reschedule their appointment through a link in the email they receive.

## Email notifications

Confirmations and reminders are sent by email to both the patient and the clinic.
